A complete eco-conscious artisan e-commerce theme for WordPress and WooCommerce — tactile minimalism, botanical greens and a one-click setup wizard.

== Description ==

GV Craft is a production-ready WooCommerce theme for artisan and sustainable
goods marketplaces. It ships with the complete "Verdant Craft Minimal" design
system that powers the Greenvision Craft storefront:

* Editorial hero with a craft hotspot, live trust metrics and quick add
* Craft-discipline bento grid driven by your real product categories
* Curated product grid with eco badges, guild names and instant AJAX add-to-bag
* Craft Impact Ledger dashboard (4 live, editable metrics)
* Artisan Guild Spotlight, brand guarantee bar and regenerative journal feed
* Designed shop archive with craft filters, price range and eco certification tags
* Full product detail page: gallery, variation picker, eco ledger, colourway
  swatches, provenance story, materials bento and artisan reviews
* Cart, checkout and account pages rebuilt on the design system
* Mobile bottom navigation, off-canvas drawer, wishlist and toast feedback

== Installation ==

1. Upload the `gv-craft` folder to `/wp-content/themes/` (or install the ZIP
   through Appearance → Themes → Add New → Upload Theme).
2. Activate the theme.
3. Install and activate WooCommerce (Appearance → Themes → Add New → WooCommerce).
4. Open **Appearance → GV Craft Setup**.
5. Click **Run the setup** — this creates every page, wires the WooCommerce
   Shop / Cart / Checkout / My Account pages and builds the primary, footer and
   legal menus.
6. Click **Import demo content** — adds the artisan catalogue, craft categories
   and journal posts so the homepage is immediately alive.
7. If WooCommerce shows the "Coming soon" placeholder, launch the store from
   WooCommerce → Settings → General → *Store visibility*.

== Frequently Asked Questions ==

= Does it work without WooCommerce? =

Yes. The theme degrades gracefully: the blog, pages, journal and about pages all
render, and the shop sections are simply skipped.

= Can I change every homepage string? =

Yes. Appearance → Customize → GV Craft Options. The announcement bar, hero,
discipline grid, impact ledger, guild spotlight, guarantees, newsletter and
footer are all editable, with live preview.

= How do I add eco badges and artisan stories to a product? =

Edit the product and fill in the **GV Craft — Eco Story** panel: eco badges,
artisan guild, batch number, three impact metrics, the material story,
colourways and provenance image. They appear on the card and the product page.

= How do I change the colours? =

Appearance → Customize → GV Craft Options → Accent Colours. The full palette is
also exposed to the block editor through theme.json.
